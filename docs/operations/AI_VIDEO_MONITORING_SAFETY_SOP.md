# AI Video Monitoring Safety SOP

Last updated: 2026-07-05

This SOP defines how AI-assisted video monitoring may be used to support RBT oversight and child safety. AI is an alerting aid only. It is not a clinician, emergency responder, or substitute for required supervision.

## Approved Use

AI may flag:

- Child out of camera frame.
- RBT out of frame during active session.
- Possible fall or abrupt movement.
- Long periods with no visible interaction.
- Elevated distress signal.
- Session note missing after a video alert.
- Unauthorized person in therapy area.
- Camera blocked, muted, or disconnected.

AI must not:

- Diagnose.
- Decide treatment changes.
- Decide emergency response alone.
- Score clinical progress without BCBA review.
- Replace RBT supervision requirements.
- Replace parent/guardian consent.
- Release claims by itself.

## Consent Requirements

Before video monitoring:

- Parent/guardian consent signed.
- Consent explains whether video is live-only or recorded.
- Consent explains AI-assisted alerting.
- Consent explains who can view video/clips.
- Consent explains retention period.
- Consent explains how to revoke consent.
- Consent is stored in the record.

## Alert Severity

| Severity | Examples | Response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High | Child out of room/frame, possible fall, severe distress, unsafe object, RBT absent | Immediate RBT confirmation, BCBA notification, parent/guardian as appropriate |
| Medium | Rising distress, repeated non-response, camera quality issue | RBT check-in, BCBA review within same day |
| Low | Missing note, short camera interruption, routine quality flag | Resolve before claim release or end-of-day QA |

## High-Risk Alert Workflow

1. AI creates alert.
2. System notifies live RBT and BCBA.
3. RBT confirms child status.
4. If immediate risk exists, RBT follows emergency/clinic protocol.
5. BCBA reviews clip or live feed.
6. Incident note is created if needed.
7. Parent/guardian communication is documented if needed.
8. Claim is held if session documentation is incomplete or safety event is unresolved.

## Storage and Retention

Default recommendation:

- Live monitoring preferred over routine recording.
- Store only alert clips where operationally necessary.
- Keep retention short unless payer, legal, incident, or clinical rules require longer.
- Restrict clip access to Owner BCBA, BCBA B, and specifically authorized QA staff.
- Log every video view and download.

## AI Vendor Requirements

Before using an AI/video vendor:

- BAA signed.
- Encryption in transit and at rest.
- MFA and role-based access.
- Audit logs available.
- Data retention configurable.
- PHI not used for model training unless explicitly approved.
- Ability to delete/export data.
- Incident reporting obligations documented.

## Human Review Rule

Every high or medium alert requires human review. The final decision must be made by the appropriate RBT, BCBA, parent/guardian, or emergency responder depending on the situation.
